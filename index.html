About

I'm a graduate student at Carnegie Mellon University Africa in Kigali, studying Electrical and Computer Engineering with a concentration in AI and Machine Learning.

My research covers retrieval-augmented generation, reinforcement learning, natural language processing and computer vision. I turn that work into systems people can use: a biomedical question-answering pipeline, a crop segmentation model for smallholder farmers, and a robot control policy trained in simulation.

Before graduate school I worked in telecom and software. I helped run a national mobile network and built web products for businesses and NGOs, so I care about systems that are reliable as well as accurate.

Hierarchical goal-conditioned RL for RAG

August 2026 to present

An adaptive retrieval-augmented question-answering system. The agent decides how to retrieve and answer using Semi-Markov Decision Processes and hierarchical goal-conditioned reinforcement learning. Training runs on HotpotQA, with evaluation planned on MuSiQue, Natural Questions and AmbigQA.

Entropy-guided mean-reverting temperature

Course research project · Feb to Apr 2026

A training-free decoding method that treats sampling temperature as an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process steered by token entropy. It raised Llama-3-8B accuracy on GSM8K from 40.1% to 53.4%.

Computer Vision Lab · Nov 2025 to May 2026

I led a project that fuses Sentinel-2 time series with UAV imagery, combining spectral and phenological detail with fine spatial resolution to help smallholder farmers monitor crops. Datasets: PASTIS, PASTIS-R and MTLCC.

May 2026

Goal-conditioned SAC for a 7-DOF pick-and-place arm. Success rose from 6% to 95% with hindsight experience replay, and stayed at 93% to 94% with domain randomization.
